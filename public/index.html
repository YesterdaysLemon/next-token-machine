The Next-Token Machine

A tiny pretend language model, drawn in pixels. Text gets chopped into tokens, pushed up through attention and MLP layers, turned into odds for the next token, and one gets picked. Then that token is fed back in and the whole machine runs again.

    TOY VS. THE REAL THING

    Every step on the screen is a real step an LLM takes, in the right order. The sizes are shrunk to fit on a screen, and the next-token odds here come from a small hand-written table rather than from learned weights. The vectors and attention patterns are computed with random matrices, so they move like the real thing but don't mean anything.
